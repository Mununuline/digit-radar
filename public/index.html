    How to read a scan

    A scan reads the latest ticks of the market you picked and reports which digit, barrier or side has run furthest from fair odds.

    Strength compares that gap with normal random variation. The scan picks the best-looking digit or barrier from the same data, so Weak is the usual result and some Moderate results appear by chance alone.

    A result expires after 20 seconds because the sample moves with every tick. Scan again for a fresh one.

    Deriv synthetic indices are random. A scan describes what already happened and cannot tell you the next digit. Use the Analysis tab, the paper test and the journal before you risk money.

    Past ticks describe what already happened. They cannot tell you what the next tick will be, and a digit contract can lose the whole stake. Trade only what you can afford to lose.
